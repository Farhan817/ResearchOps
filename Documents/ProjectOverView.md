# Research Collaboration and Experiment Tracking

## Problem Statement

Research teams often maintain experimental data, observations, documents, and results across multiple spreadsheets, emails, and disconnected storage systems.

This makes it difficult to access existing research information, track experiments, and maintain visibility into ongoing research activities.

## Target Customers

- Universities
- Research Laboratories
- Research Organizations
- Pharmaceutical Companies

## Proposed Solution

A centralized platform that helps research organizations manage experiments, research data, expenses, and approvals in one place.

## Features

### 1. Experiment Repository

Users will be able to create, manage, and view experiments in a centralized repository.

The repository will provide visibility into ongoing and previously conducted experiments, helping researchers identify whether similar experiments have already been performed or if the desired results are already available.

**Main Benefits:**

- Avoid repeating experiments unnecessarily.
- Save the time and materials invested in previously conducted research.
- Make experiment records and results readily available.
- Provide easy access to existing research information.

The main objective is to ensure that the time, effort, and materials invested in every experiment are documented and accessible for future reference.

### 2. Expense Management

Users will be able to add and view expenses associated with research activities and individual experiments.

One of the major challenges in research is tracking finances, especially when multiple experiments are conducted simultaneously. Expenses can easily be missed when financial records are maintained separately.

Missed expenses can quickly affect research budgets and disrupt ongoing research activities.

**Main Benefits:**

- Maintain a centralized record of research expenses.
- Associate expenses with individual experiments.
- Make expense information readily available.
- Reduce the chances of missing research-related expenses.
- Improve visibility into research finances.

The main objective is to ensure that all research-related expenses are properly recorded and easily accessible.

### 3. Dashboard

A simple, consolidated dashboard providing a quick overview of research activities and associated expenses through data visualizations.

The dashboard will help users understand the current state of research activities and identify potential anomalies.

**Main Benefits:**

- Provide a consolidated view of research activities.
- Display a quick overview of ongoing experiments.
- Visualize research expenses.
- Help identify unusual spending patterns or anomalies.
- Make important information easily accessible.
